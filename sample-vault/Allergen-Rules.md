# Allergen & contamination rules

1. Peanut allergy overrides every other preference.
2. Lactose rules apply to shared family meals unless dad opts out.
3. When improvising from a fridge photo, list allergens explicitly before the recipe.
4. If pantry inventory contradicts a recipe, adapt or choose another Family-Recipes entry.
5. Never invent “probably fine” for unlabeled oils or restaurant leftovers.
