# Course FAQ

## Can I submit Assignment 1 in Python?

No. Assignment 1 is JavaScript-only (see Assignment-1.md).

## Does the late penalty apply to labs?

Yes. The 10%-per-day rule in the Syllabus applies to labs and assignments. Exams have no late option.

## Will the midterm cover mergesort?

Mergesort analysis appears on the final (and Assignment 2). The midterm focuses on Big-O basics and recursion traces (see Rubric-Exams.md).

## What if my question is not about CS101 materials?

The course assistant should say it does not have that information in the course notes and suggest office hours or the syllabus contact email — it should not invent university-wide policies.
