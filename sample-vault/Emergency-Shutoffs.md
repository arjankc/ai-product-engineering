# Emergency Shutoffs

For active flooding, close the nearest safe water valve and contact the landlord. For sparks, burning smell, shock, or a hot electrical panel, do not touch wet surfaces; switch off power only if safely accessible and call an electrician.

For suspected LPG leak, do not operate switches or flames. Ventilate if safe, leave the area, close the cylinder valve only if safe, and contact emergency help or the supplier.
