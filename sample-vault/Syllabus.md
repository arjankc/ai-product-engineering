## Grading

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Exams (midterm + final) | 50% |
| Labs | 30% |
| Assignments | 20% |

## Policies

- Late work: **10% penalty per day**, capped at 50%. After 5 days the submission is not accepted.
- Collaboration: discuss ideas freely; submitted code and write-ups must be your own.
- AI tools: allowed for brainstorming; you must disclose use in the README of each assignment.
