# Router Manual

The router is a NetGate Home X2 at `192.168.1.1`. A solid blue internet LED means the WAN link is healthy; blinking amber means negotiation is in progress.

## Safe recovery

1. Check power and WAN cable seating.
2. Wait two minutes after a normal reboot.
3. Never hold Reset unless configuration backup has been confirmed; a factory reset erases the ISP and Wi-Fi settings.
