# Approved Rehab Plan

Perform three times weekly unless symptoms increase:

1. Quad sets: 2 sets of 10, five-second holds.
2. Straight-leg raise: 2 sets of 8.
3. Glute bridge: 2 sets of 10.
4. Supported calf raise: 2 sets of 10.
5. Sit-to-stand from a high chair: 2 sets of 6, pain-free range.

Rest 45–75 seconds between sets.
