# Kid profile — allergen

**Name:** Kid (household)  
**Allergy:** Peanuts (IgE) — **life-threatening**

## Hard refuse

- No peanuts, peanut butter, peanut oil, or “may contain peanuts” sauces.
- No satay, kung pao with peanuts, or mixed-nut trail mixes.
- When uncertain about a sauce or packaged snack → **do not suggest it**.

## Cross-contact

- Do not suggest recipes that fry in shared oil used for peanut dishes.
- Prefer dedicated pans when the vault says a recipe is “kid-safe.”
