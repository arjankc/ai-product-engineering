# Family recipes (trusted)

## Grandma’s tomato pasta (kid-safe variant)

- Penne, olive oil, garlic, canned tomato, spinach.
- No cream; no cheese unless lactose-free sprinkle for dad only at the table.
- Never add peanut-based chili crisp.

## Weeknight chicken rice bowl

- Cooked rice, chicken thighs, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, carrots.
- Serve spinach on the side lightly wilted.
- Kid-safe if soy sauce brand is peanut-free (verify label).

## Lentil dal + rice

- Lentils, onion, garlic, turmeric, rice.
- Finish with lactose-free milk splash only if needed — default is water/stock.
