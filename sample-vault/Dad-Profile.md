# Dad profile — dietary needs

**Name:** Dad (household)  
**Restriction:** Lactose intolerance

## Hard rules

- No milk, cream, soft cheese, ice cream, or butter-heavy sauces unless labeled lactose-free.
- Hard aged cheese in tiny amounts is sometimes tolerated — **ask before suggesting**.
- Prefer oat milk or lactose-free milk when a recipe needs “milk.”

## Safe favorites

- Dal, rice, stir-fried vegetables, grilled chicken, lactose-free yogurt if available.
